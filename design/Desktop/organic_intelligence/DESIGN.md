## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined intersection between natural wellness and considerate intelligence. Designed for an organic grocery assistant, the aesthetic discards clinical, synthetic tech tropes in favor of an artisanal yet hyper-competent digital atmosphere.

### Personality & Tone
- **Wholesome & Grounded:** Rooted in soil, sunlight, and clean nutrition without leaning into rustic cliché.
- **Intelligent & Unhurried:** Clear, structured data delivery that eases decision-making and reduces cognitive load during meal planning and cart management.
- **Warm & Trustworthy:** Reassuring, high-legibility typographic balance paired with nourishing, earth-derived accents.

### Design Movement
**Modern Organic Tactility:** A synthesis of Scandinavian culinary minimalism and structured conversational commerce. Layouts prioritize generous breathing room, calm low-contrast outlines over aggressive drop shadows, and soft ambient warmth that feels closer to an artisanal pantry inventory than a utilitarian database.

## Colors

The color system relies on natural foliage, golden culinary highlights, and a warm cream foundation to create an appetite-conscious, reliable digital retail space.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` - Deep Botanical Pine):** Carries the definitive authority of the brand. Applied to key interactive controls, primary conversational prompts, essential headings, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary (`#2D7A54` - Fresh Leaf Green):** Represents freshness, organic verification tags, affirmative micro-interactions, and active state highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Honey Amber):** An appetite-inducing accent utilized for seasonal alerts, dietary warnings, savings tags, AI synthesis highlights, and dynamic cart notifications.
- **Neutral Background (`#FBF9F5` - Spelt Cream):** A non-glare, comforting off-white canvas that softens screen intensity and mimics premium unbleached packaging paper.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Bone):** Used exclusively for modular content cards, floating chat trays, and product containers to establish discrete spatial layers against the cream background.
- **Borders & Rules (`#E9E4DC` - Linen Fiber):** Subtle, organic dividers that provide structure without high-contrast friction.
- **Text & Content Tone:** Primary typography is anchored in `#1E293B` (Deep Slate Slate) for sharp legibility, supported by `#475569` (Muted Herb Slate) for metadata, assistant notes, and secondary details.

## Typography

The typography system relies solely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, friendly open counters, and high-performance legibility across conversational viewports and product spec listings.

### Usage Principles
- **Headlines:** Use weights `600` and `700` with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to ground conversational summaries and aisle categorizations with authority.
- **Body:** Kept at weight `400` with extended line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to maximize readability when the AI outputs complex recipe substitutions or multi-item dietary analyses.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Leverage weight `600` with neutral or slight positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.02em`) to make nutritional badges, dietary micro-tags, and pricing clear at smaller scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a hybrid workspace combining a flexible conversational split-view with an adaptive product showcase grid.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fixed canvas max-width of `1440px` centered. Dual-pane layout featuring an adaptive AI Assistant panel (persisting between `380px` and `440px`) pinned alongside an 8-column product/cart workspace with `gutter-desktop` (`2rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (`1.25rem`) and `margin-tablet` (`2rem`). Conversational agent docks into an expandable persistent bottom sheet or slide-over drawer.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column fluid viewport with `margin` (`1rem`) and `gutter` (`1rem`). The interface toggles smoothly between conversational chat cards and a consolidated horizontal-scroll tray for recommended items.

### Spacing Model
- Maintain strict alignment using the `space-*` scale for element stacks.
- Inner card padding uses `space-md` for standard catalog cards and `space-lg` for conversational thought bubble outputs and comparison matrices.
- Section separators use `space-xl` to ensure cognitive isolation between recipe discovery, active cart audits, and suggested dietary alternatives.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering and botanical-tinted ambient shadows rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing the organic atmosphere.

### Spatial Levels
- **Base Canvas:** `#FBF9F5` establishes the foundational warm plane.
- **Surface Level 1 (Static Cards & Containers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with a delicate outline (`1px solid #E9E4DC`). No shadow is required here; clarity comes from pure tonal contrast against the cream canvas.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Modules & Chat Bubbles):** `#FFFFFF` supported by an ambient botanical shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Floating Cart Trays, Tooltips, & Context Menus):** Elevated white surfaces with a deeper dual shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.03)` with a `1px` subtle border in `#E9E4DC`.
- **Surface Level 4 (Modal Backdrops & Overlays):** `#1E293B` applied at `40%` opacity accompanied by a gentle `blur(4px)` backdrop filter, preserving underlying context without causing visual noise.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric curvature tier `2` (`0.5rem` base), generating soft, approachable components that reflect organic shapes without drifting into juvenile geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Elements (Badges, Inline Inputs, Small Buttons):** `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`).
- **Standard Cards, Chat Panels, & Trays:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) to gently enclose conversational dialogs and product listings.
- **Hero Comparison Matrices & Major Drawers:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) for high-level structure.
- **Nutritional Chips & Interactive Pills:** Fully circular radii (`9999px`) to facilitate rapid tactile scanning and tap-selection.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1B4D3E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, bold `label-lg` typography. Hover states subtly lift using a background shift to `#2D7A54`.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill with `1.5px` border in `#1B4D3E`, text in `#1B4D3E`. Hover fills with `#F4F1EA`.
- **Tertiary Accent (Checkout / Instant Purchase):** Solid `#D97706` fill, `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for high-intent conversions and cart progressions.

### Conversational UI Cards & Chat Trays
- **User Prompts:** Right-aligned, colored with `#2D7A54` tint (`8%` opacity) over `#FFFFFF`, crisp border `#E9E4DC`, radius `1rem` with bottom-right corner docked (`0.25rem`).
- **Assistant Responses:** Left-aligned, `#FFFFFF` card, subtle ambient shadow, radius `1rem` with top-left corner docked. Features an organic botanical avatar accent in `#1B4D3E`.

### Agent Trace Panels (Reasoning Engine)
- Foldable, accordion-style micro-panels with `#F4F1EA` background and a `1px dashed #E9E4DC` border.
- Uses `body-sm` font with `#475569` to show transparent dietary filtering steps (e.g., *"Checked 14 items for non-GMO certification and soy allergens"*). Includes a pulsating status dot in `#2D7A54`.

### Comparison Matrices
- Clean, structured column tables built inside a `rounded-lg` white surface.
- Alternating attribute rows use `#FBF9F5` (50% opacity).
- Recommended items feature a top banner badge in `#D97706` with text *"Best Organic Value"*.

### Dietary & Product Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded at `0.25rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal.
- **Unselected:** Border `1px solid #E9E4DC`, `#FFFFFF` background, `#475569` text.
- **Active:** Solid `#1B4D3E` background, `#FFFFFF` text with a leading botanical checkmark.

### Form Inputs
- Height `44px`, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E9E4DC` border, `0.5rem` radius.
- Focus state reveals a `2px` ring in `#2D7A54` with zero drop-shadow spread, maintaining visual calmness.

### Cart & Checkout Elements
- Item list features micro-steppers for quantity adjustment, embedded in a unified container with clear removal actions.
- Cart totals split ingredient costs and highlight calculated savings derived from organic bulk substitutes using `#D97706`.
